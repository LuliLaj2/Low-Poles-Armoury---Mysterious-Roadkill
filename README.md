# Low-Poles-Armoury---Mysterious-Roadkill
The scene features a road through the woods and the scene of an accident in which a person was hit by a wagon.  It works well as an encounter - whether your players stumbled upon the scene or the ones responsible. 

The map also features a version without the roadkill, which turns it into just a road in the woods with a couple of places of interest.

The map also includes a small camp, a roadside shrine and a fairy circle.
