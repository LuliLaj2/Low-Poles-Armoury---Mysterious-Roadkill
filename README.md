Mysterious Roadkill - Description
You're on a road in the woods. And in the middle of the road, a wagon seems to have hit someone

This map depicts an accidental roadkill scene used as an encounter; players may stumble upon it or even cause the accident. There is also a version without the wagon person hit, which turns this scene into a simple road-through-the-forest kind of encounter.

The wagon seems to be a regular inter-city transportation unit, with a couple of additional seats added via a cart attached to the back. Obviously, the wagon hit a victim while they were exiting dense roadside vegetation.

Places of interest:
Roadside shrine of the Cat-Mother:




Roadside shrines are common in this region. These curved roads are often travelled on foot or in small vehicles, so adventurers often stop near these shrines to pray and leave gifts for protection on the road.

This shrine honours the Cat-Mother, representing Sharess in her cat form, also known as the Mother of Cats. Sharess is the chaotic good goddess of hedonism, cats, and sensual fulfilment. She has a fully feline temperament and can transform into a beautiful cat or cat-human hybrid.

People often place flowers and food at the cat's paws to request safe, discreet travel along this road.

The fey and planar spirits that watch over the shrine are incredibly protective. Anyone who steals from the shrine, or harms a feline within a mile of this location, suffers the Curse of the Bad Omen. For the next three days, they suffer horrible luck (disadvantage on all Stealth and Perception checks). During that time, every cat they meet hisses at them.

Fairy Circle:

A blue circle of mushrooms is located behind the shrine, surrounded by trees. Feys may come from it to atack anyone who damages the cat statue or the shrine itself.

Small Camp:

The person who was hit by the wagon was camping here, probably wanting to make a rest near the shrine. He was carrying a flower to place on the shrine before he was hit.
